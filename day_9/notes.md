# Day 9 - Prompt Engineering

## What is Prompt Engineering?
Writing smart instructions to get best results from AI.

## 3 Types of Prompting:

### 1. Zero-Shot Prompting
- No examples given
- Just ask the question directly
- Example: "Classify this sentiment: I love this!"

### 2. Few-Shot Prompting
- Give 2-3 examples first
- Then ask your question
- AI learns from your examples

### 3. Chain of Thought (CoT)
- Ask AI to think step by step
- Best for math and logic problems
- Use: "Let's think step by step"

## Marketing Prompts
- Always define: Role, Task, Tone, Length
- Example: "You are a copywriter. Write a caption..."

## Key Learnings
- Better prompt = Better AI output
- Always give context to AI
- Step by step prompts give best results
